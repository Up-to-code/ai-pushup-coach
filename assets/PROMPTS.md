## 💡 Tips for LLM Refinement
- **Color Correction:** If the AI generates too much color, add `--no gradients, shadows` to the prompt.
- **Consistency:** Always specify "Isolated on #0D0F14 background" to ensure easy transparency removal for the app.
- **Aspect Ratio:** For card backgrounds, use `--ar 2:1`. For badges, use `--ar 1:1`.
