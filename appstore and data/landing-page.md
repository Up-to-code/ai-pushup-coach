## Overview

Push Counter helps you build a stronger push-up habit with guided plans, live rep tracking, workout history, and progress feedback. Start with your current level, choose realistic training days, set a goal, and follow a four-week plan built around your schedule.

During workouts, use camera-assisted rep counting on supported devices or continue manually when camera access is unavailable. Push Counter keeps your reps, sets, rest time, reminders, streaks, challenges, and leaderboard motivation in one focused fitness experience.

## Features

| Feature | Website Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Live Rep Tracking | Use the camera during workouts to estimate push-up movement, count reps, and surface simple tracking guidance. |
| Personal Training Plans | Build a four-week push-up plan around your level, goal, training days, and preferred workout time. |
| Practice Modes | Choose Sets Workout, Open Goal, Reps Per Minute, or Daily Session for flexible training outside your plan. |
| Workout History | Track completed workouts, reps, duration, estimated calories, streaks, best sessions, badges, and weekly progress. |
| Challenges and Leaderboards | Compare progress with global, country, friends, and challenge-based ranking surfaces when signed in. |
| Privacy-Aware Camera Use | Camera access is requested only for live workout tracking, and workout video is not saved to the photo library by default. |
