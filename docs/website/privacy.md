# AI Push-Up Coach Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026

App page: https://nexfiy.com/apps/ai-pushup-coach

AI Push-Up Coach helps users track push-up workouts, training plans, progress history, and optional social ranking features. This policy explains the practical data the app uses for those features.

## Information We Use

- Profile information you provide or sync through sign-in, such as display name, nickname, bio, country, and coach tone.
- Workout information, such as reps, duration, calories, workout type, goal, sets, completion state, form feedback state, camera mode, quality score, and workout dates.
- Training settings, such as workout days, reminder time, sound, haptics, camera mode, and notification preferences.
- Subscription status from RevenueCat, such as whether the Pro entitlement is active.
- Social and leaderboard data when you use synced features, such as follows, friends, country rank, challenge progress, and notifications.

## Camera Use

The camera is used during live workout sessions to estimate push-up movement and count reps. Camera access is requested when entering the camera workout flow. The app does not need microphone audio for workout tracking.

Workout telemetry related to camera tracking may be saved, such as camera presentation state, form feedback state, tracking issues, quality score, and completed workout results. This supports progress history, troubleshooting, and leaderboard features.

## Local Storage and Sync

The app stores core workout, profile, settings, and onboarding data locally on your device so workouts can continue even when network services are unavailable.

When you are signed in and backend configuration is available, selected profile, settings, workout, social, challenge, and leaderboard data may sync with Convex-backed services. Guest or offline use is local-first and may not include synced social features.

## Purchases

Subscription purchase and restore flows are handled through RevenueCat and the App Store. AI Push-Up Coach uses subscription status to unlock Pro features, but payment details are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.

## Notifications

Workout reminders and missed-workout follow-ups are scheduled locally based on your selected workout plan and reminder settings. You can disable notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.

## Your Choices

- You can edit your profile, country, coach tone, and training settings in the app.
- You can deny camera permission; camera-based counting will not work without camera access.
- You can use the app in guest/offline mode for local-first training flows.
- You can manage or restore subscriptions through the app when subscription services are available.
- You can reset local onboarding or change notification settings from Settings.
