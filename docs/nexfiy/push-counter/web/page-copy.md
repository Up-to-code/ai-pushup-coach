## Overview

Push Counter helps you build a stronger push-up habit with guided plans, live rep tracking, workout history, and progress feedback. Use the camera during workouts for movement-based counting, train manually when camera access is unavailable, and keep your routine moving with local reminders, streaks, challenges, and leaderboard motivation.

## Features

Live Rep Tracking

Use the camera during workouts to estimate push-up movement, count reps, and surface simple form feedback.

Personal Training Plans

Build a plan around your level, goal, training days, and preferred workout time.

Workout History

Track completed workouts, reps, duration, calories, streaks, best sessions, and weekly progress.

Challenges and Leaderboards

Compare progress with global, country, friend, and challenge-based ranking surfaces when signed in.

Local-First Training

Use guest mode and local workout history for core training flows, with optional account sync for social and cross-device features.

Privacy-Aware Camera Use

Camera access is requested only for live workout tracking, and workout video is not saved to the photo library by default.
